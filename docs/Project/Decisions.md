# Decisions

The locked design decisions, newest first: the first ones while the language was designed (June 2026), the later ones as it grew (to D115 in November 2026). They are the "why" behind the rules on the [[Language]] pages. A decision is settled: changing one means adding a new numbered decision that supersedes it, not editing the old row.

Links in the early rows point at the design documents in [[Archive]], where those decisions are discussed in detail; the later rows link their tasks.

## Adding a decision

Add a row at the top with the next number (D116, …), today's date, the decision and the reason, and update the affected [[Language]] or [[Architecture]] pages. Decisions that tasks need are referenced from the task (for example [[MYLA-6]] asks for one on float-to-integer casts).

| D87 | 2026-10-29 | **Guaranteed tail calls: `become`** ([[MYLA-128]], details: [[Language/Statements]]).
