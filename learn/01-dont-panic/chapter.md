Welcome aboard. Every exercise in this course is a small Myla program that doesn't work yet: it doesn't compile, prints the wrong thing, or fails its tests. Fix it, press **Check**, and collect your XP.

A few things to know before the Vogons arrive:

- A program starts at `fun main()`. Statements end at the end of the line; no semicolons needed.
- `print(x)` prints one value and a newline.
- `let` names a value that never changes; `var` one that may.
- Functions say what they return, and must `return` it.

The [[Language/Statements]] page has the details, if you're the kind of hitchhiker who reads the manual.
