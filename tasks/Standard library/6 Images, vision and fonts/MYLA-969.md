## Evidence

Measured on 2026-11-25 in the lane's worktree (branch `stdlib/MYLA-969` over `088e285`), on a shared 4-core x86-64 machine.

- **API** ([[Language/Standard-library/Font]]): `parse(b, index = 0) -> Result<Font, FontError>`, `face_count(b)`; `Font { family, style, units_per_em, ascender, descender, line_gap, glyph_count, … }` with `glyph(c)`, `advance(g)`, `lsb(g)`, `kerning(a, b)`, `outline(g) -> Option<draw.Path>`, `scale(px)`; `FontError { Truncated, BadSignature, MissingTable(str), Unsupported(str), Corrupt(str), NoFace(int) }` (its own enum, not `ImageError`: a font's errors name tables and faces); `render_glyph(f, g, px, dx = 0.0) -> draw.Mask` placed from the pen on the baseline; `layout(f, text, px, opts) -> TextLayout { glyphs: [Placed], width, height, lines, px }`, `measure`, `LayoutOptions { max_width, align, line_height, tab, kerning }` with `with_*` copies; `glyph_cache(f) -> GlyphCache` with `masks(l, x, y)`, `mask(g, px, x, y)`, `count()`; on `Canvas`: `fill_masks(ms, paint)` and `fill_text(f, text, x, y, px, paint)` (`var self` methods std.font adds, so the canvas isn't copied).
- **Module cases** (`lib/std/font.my`, 53: `#test` cases on the public functions and methods with values, test functions on a 6-glyph font the module builds for its tests: format 4 cmap through the glyph array and by delta, a format 12 face in a `.ttc`, short and long `loca`, repeated flags and short coordinates, all-off-curve contours, a scaled composite and one placed by point matching, trailing left side bearings, Mac Roman names, OS/2 typo metrics, kerning, layouts with wrapping, alignment, tabs, line height and control characters, the cache's quarter pixels, drawing): `build/myla test lib/std/font.my --stdlib lib` 53/53 (13 s); `--native` 53/53; `--target aarch64-linux` (qemu) 53/53; `--target wasm32-wasi` with `MYLA_EMULATOR="node tools/wasm/run.mjs"` 53/53. A test function also parses every prefix of that font and every one of its bytes inverted, and renders and lays out what parses.
- **Burn test** `python3 -m unittest tests.burn.test_font` (driver `tests/burn/drivers/font_driver.my`): 14 tests OK in 60 s, the fuzzing included. Against a TrueType reader in Python: both faces' names and metrics, the 176 glyphs' advances, bearings and outline boxes (= their `glyf` boxes, the 49 composites included), all 163 `cmap` characters and 7 the font lacks, all 104 kerning pairs and 52 non-pairs. Against the 48 unhinted FreeType 2.13.2 renderings (A, g, @, é at 12/24/48 px, offsets 0, ¼, ½, ¾): the same bitmap box for all 48, at most 5 levels a pixel, mean at most 0.37 (A ≤ 2, é ≤ 1, g and @ ≤ 5); advances equal to FreeType's to 1/65536 px. The two strings, each glyph at its exact fraction: at most 5 levels (means 0.14, 0.33); through the glyph cache at quarter pixels: at most 33 (means 1.3, 2.2). Every glyph's 48 px mask covers its curves' exact area within perimeter × 1/16 px. 12 fixed and 200 random layouts match a Python layout of the documented rules to 0.001 px. Interpreted and native answers identical on a sample.
- **Failing first**: with std.draw's default flattening (a quarter pixel) the curved glyphs were up to 65 levels off FreeType, at 0.05 px still 16 and at 0.01 px 21 (FreeType's own cutting of curves is the difference): cutting quadratics as `gray_render_conic` does, on FreeType's 1/64 and 1/256 pixel grids, brought it to 5. The strings were up to 255 levels off until it showed that the references aren't kerned (FreeType gave no kerning for the font's `kern` pairs; unkerned pens match to 2 levels); I corrected the two entries' notes in `manifest.json` and `_images_vendor.py`, and the wording in `_images.py` and [[Development/Testing]], and added `LayoutOptions.kerning` so the test lays them out the same way. Two expectations of my own module tests were wrong at first (a curve's top is half way to its control point).
- **Fuzzing** (`test_fuzz_natively`, `test_fuzz_checked`): every truncation of `MylaTestSans.ttf` and `.ttc` up to 512 bytes, every 17th length after that (2,673 more), and 10,000 seeded mutations (`_images.fuzz`, seed 945), each parsed in every face, every glyph outlined and rendered at 12 px and a wrapped, centred text with a tab and an astral character laid out and drawn through a cache: all `ok` or `err`, natively plain (31 s) and `--checked` (34 s), and plain under qemu-aarch64 (`MYLA_TEST_TARGET=aarch64-linux`, 519 s); the glyph, string, layout and parsing tests also pass on aarch64.
- **Visual check against Pillow**: the seven Pillow (autohinted, whole-pixel) renderings next to ours, kerned at their fractional pens, looked at side by side: the same shapes, our strokes softer where hinting snaps Pillow's to pixels, the same ink within 5 %.

## Review (2026-11-27)

- A composite's `SCALED_COMPONENT_OFFSET` scaled x by the length of the matrix's column (xx, yx) and y by (xy, yy); FreeType scales x by its row (xx, xy) and y by (yx, yy). With a 2 by 2 that isn't symmetric, the component was hundreds of units off: FreeType 2.14 (through Pillow) puts the test's first composite at x 524, std.font put it at 400. `scaled_component_offsets` failed before.
- `kerning(a, b)` with a glyph number past the font's could find another pair, its key `(a << 16) | b` shared (`kerning(0, 131075)` gave `(2, 3)`'s -50). Glyphs past the font now kern by 0.
- Module cases 55/55 interpreted and `--native`.
