- **Owns:** `lib/std/jpeg.my` (the decoder), `docs/Language/Standard-library/Jpeg.md`
- **Needs:** [[MYLA-959]], [[MYLA-957]], [[MYLA-971]]
- Work by the lane rules of [[Project/Standard-library-roadmap#Working in parallel]]: touch only the files this task owns, plus one row of the index table on [[Language/Standard-library]]; don't commit seeds or `selfhost/embedded_std.my`.

- [x] Markers and segments: SOI, APPn, DQT (8 and 16 bit), SOF0/1/2, DHT, SOS, DRI, RSTn, EOI; a segment's length checked against the input before it is read
- [x] Baseline: Huffman decoding, every sampling factor, restart intervals, libjpeg's islow IDCT and its fancy upsampling, YCbCr → RGB by libjpeg's fixed-point tables
- [x] The baseline corpus decodes bit-identical to Pillow's (vendored PAMs), on every runner
- [x] Progressive: spectral selection and successive approximation (DC and AC, first and refining scans), the same exactness
- [x] Adobe APP14 CMYK and YCCK to RGB; `JpegMeta` (JFIF density, EXIF, ICC); `orientation`, `apply_orientation` (YCCK follows libjpeg's conversion but no file in the corpus has it)
- [x] Arithmetic coding, lossless, 12-bit and hierarchical are `Unsupported(…)` by name
- [x] Measured natively: a 12-megapixel baseline and a progressive photo, beside Pillow (libjpeg-turbo); a profile of where the time goes
- [x] Its corpus and fuzz cases in [[MYLA-971]]'s harness: truncations and seeded mutations are `Ok` or `Err`, never a runtime error, natively plain and `--checked`
- [x] `#test` cases on every public function that has values to compare (images up to about 16×16), test functions for the generic ones (D162), passing interpreted and `--native`, and on aarch64 (qemu) and wasm32 (Node)

## Evidence

Branch `stdlib/MYLA-962` over `088e285`, 2026-11-25.

- **Exactness:** `tests/burn/drivers/jpeg.my` over the corpus: all 31 `ok` JPEGs equal to Pillow 13's pixels (libjpeg-turbo 3.2), every byte, the first time the whole decoder ran: 4:4:4, 4:2:2, 4:2:0, 4:4:0 (h1v2 fancy), 4:1:1 (replicated, as libjpeg), 1x1 (too narrow for the triangle filter), 61x47 partial MCUs, gray, three progressive files (spectral selection, successive approximation, restart markers, optimised tables), restart markers every block and every row, Adobe CMYK, EXIF orientations. The 11 broken files: the 9 `err` ones are `Err` (`BadSignature`, `Truncated`, `TooLarge(4294836225)`, `Corrupt("width 0")`, `Unsupported("arithmetic coding")`, `Unsupported("lossless JPEG")`, `Unsupported("12-bit samples")`, `Corrupt("Huffman table counts")`), the 2 `any` ones images (decoded on with zero bits, as libjpeg). Beyond the corpus, three 12-MP files (4000x3000, `benchmarks/images_pillow.py`'s photo at quality 85: baseline 4:2:0, progressive 4:2:0, baseline 4:4:4) give Pillow's SHA-256 exactly. A planted mistake shows the tests catch rounding: the h2v2 filter's `+ 8` made `+ 7` fails `decodes_as_libjpeg_does`; the IDCT's final rounding made `2^17 - 1` passes the module's small cases but fails the corpus's 512x384 photo.
- **Tests:** `build/myla test lib/std/jpeg.my` 65/65 interpreted (5 s), `--native` 65/65, `--target aarch64-linux` under qemu-aarch64 65/65, `--target wasm32-wasi` under Node 65/65. `python3 -m unittest tests.burn.test_images.JpegDecoder`: 5 tests OK in 31 s (corpus natively and `--checked`, the 4 smallest interpreted, fuzz plain and `--checked` with the default 10,000 mutations); its corpus test also passes with `MYLA_TEST_TARGET=aarch64-linux` (14 s) and `wasm32-wasi`. Larger fuzz runs, 100,000 mutations each with seeds 936, 937 and 938, plain and `--checked`: the first found a bug (a DHT with too many short codes filled the lookup table past its slot before the length check: `list index 4096 out of bounds`), fixed, with a regression case in `the_tables_are_annex_k` that fails without the fix; afterwards all six runs pass (600,000 cases, each `Ok` or `Err`). `examples/jpeg_decode.my`: 7/7 cases, `test_examples_run` and `test_examples_compile` for it OK (interpreted and native output equal). `tests.burn.test_doc_blocks` OK (34 s, the new page's block included). `benchmarks/images_std.my`'s cases 15/15.
- **Measured natively** (`myla build`, bounds checks on, the fastest of 5 runs over several runs on the shared 4-core machine with other lanes running, so ±10 to 15 %): baseline 4:2:0 12 MP 400 to 430 ms (Pillow 74 ms, 5.5×), baseline 4:4:4 465 to 500 ms (Pillow 77 ms), progressive 4:2:0 650 to 780 ms (Pillow 177 ms, 3.9×). Before the speed work: 466, 513 and 762 ms. Profile of the baseline 4:2:0 decode (phases timed with std.time in a copy of the module): scan about 225 ms (allocating the planes 45, Huffman decoding about 100, IDCT about 80: the scan took 150 ms with the IDCT left out), `finish` about 210 ms (allocating the 36 MB output 80 to 100, upsampling 60, colour conversion 65). A third of the decode is `list.filled`, which pushes an element at a time ([[MYLA-949]]). Progressive: scans about 330 ms (allocating the 18 million coefficients 60 of it), IDCT 145 ms, then `finish`. What helped: the IDCT's, the upsampler's and the output's lists moved into locals while they change (`self.out[i]` in a loop reloads the field), and the colour conversion's clamp as a table (466 → 423 ms baseline, 762 → 666 ms progressive). What didn't: loading the scan's bytes by hand in the loop instead of calling `jpeg_reader`'s `refill()` (the hot loops keep `acc` and `n` in locals already) measured the same, so the module uses the reader as Bytes.md documents. `benchmarks/images_std.my` times a JPEG file named among its arguments (440 ms for the 4:2:0 one while the fuzz ran) until [[MYLA-963]]'s encoder makes the file.
- **For [[MYLA-963]]:** the shared tables are at the top of `lib/std/jpeg.my`: `natural_order()` (zigzag, with libjpeg's 16 guard entries), Annex K's `luma_quant()`, `chroma_quant()` and Huffman tables `std_huffman(luma, ac)` (checked against the DHT segments Pillow writes), IJG's `quality_scale(q)` and `scaled_quant(base, q)` (`jpeg_set_quality`'s rounding, 1 to 255), the colour section's fixed-point constants (`ycc_tables`, `clamp255`; the encoder's RGB → YCbCr goes beside them), and `Decoder.set_huffman` builds a decoding table from a DHT's counts and symbols.
- **Changed outside the owned files:** `tests/burn/drivers/jpeg.my` (the driver, 9 lines), `examples/jpeg_decode.my` with its rows in `tests/burn/test_examples_run.py` and `test_examples_compile.py`, the index row on [[Language/Standard-library]], the std.jpeg section of both copies of the skill's `reference/stdlib.md` (and `std.jpeg` in its list of modules), the JPEG line and a JPEG-file argument in `benchmarks/images_std.my`. No shared harness file changed; no manifest entry needed fixing.
- **Not done:** libjpeg's block smoothing of a progressive file whose scans leave a coefficient not fully known (a file cut between scans or an unusual script; the corpus's progressive files refine everything, so smoothing doesn't apply); YCCK is decoded by libjpeg's formulas but untested against Pillow (no file); a 2-component or 5-plus-component frame is `Unsupported` (Pillow refuses them too). Speed: SIMD-free and bounds-checked, about 5.5× libjpeg-turbo; MYLA-949 alone would take about a third off.

## Review, 2026-11-27

- **Checked against Pillow** with a coefficient-level writer (a test script, not committed) that makes files Pillow can't: any sampling factors libjpeg upsamples (3x1, 1x3, 4x2, 2x4, luma smaller than chroma, 4 components), random component ids, 8- and 16-bit DQT, optimal, standard and deep Huffman tables (codes up to 16 bits), restart intervals of 1 to 7 MCUs, sequential scans interleaved or one a component, and random progressive scripts (spectral bands, successive approximation down to 0, interleaved DC, EOB runs, refinement), JFIF, no marker, and Adobe 0, 1 and 2 (so YCCK too): about 7,000 files, every one Pillow's pixels; and 1,200 Pillow files (all qualities, subsamplings, progressive, optimize, restarts, gray, RGB, CMYK). No difference.
- **Fixed:** broken scans now decode as libjpeg does, not as zero bits to the end: once an interval's data runs out the rest of its MCUs are left (gray in a sequential scan), restart markers out of turn are resynchronised as `jpeg_resync_to_restart` does, and a component no scan reaches is gray (128) instead of 0. Test `decodes_what_is_left_as_libjpeg_does` (five broken files, Pillow's pixels); over 288 random damaged files (cuts, dropped bytes, flipped bits in a scan; Pillow with `JSIMD_FORCENONE=1`) Pillow and std.jpeg now agree on 196 where they agreed on 102 (13 were `Truncated` errors), the rest being bad Huffman codes (`Corrupt` here) and progressive files cut between scans (libjpeg's block smoothing).
- **Left:** a bit pattern that is no Huffman code is `Corrupt` (libjpeg warns and takes symbol 0 after 17 bits); libjpeg-turbo's SIMD IDCT wraps wild coefficients at 16 bits; block smoothing.
