A step of [[MYLA-165]], the Windows target, in the plan the owner accepted on 2026-11-14 ([[Project/Follow-up-plan#windows-2026-11-14]]). D211, D216: `VirtualAlloc` for the heap's chunks, `CreateThread` for the workers, `WaitOnAddress`/`WakeByAddressSingle` for parking, the TEB for each thread's state, a vectored exception handler for a guard-page fault (`runtime error: stack overflow`, status 1).

- [x] The runtime's heap, workers and parking on the Windows API
- [x] Stack overflow reported as on Linux, main thread and workers
- [x] The concurrency and stress programs pass under Wine

## What MYLA-702 left for this step

- The heap's chunks come from `VirtualAlloc` already, and go back through `VirtualFree` (`unmap_memory`): a whole mapping is released, part of one only decommitted, so the ends `std.rt.map_aligned` trims stay reserved addresses (with tasks). An aligned mapping of the platform's own (reserve, then commit the aligned part) would release them.
- Tasks run on one worker: its state block is the first of `__tls_blocks`, addressed next to `%rip` on Windows (`CodeGen.tv`, `rt_var_at`, `Intrinsic.RtAddr` in `ir_x86.my`), `__start_workers` isn't emitted and `_start` sets `__exec_workers` to 1. Workers need each thread's block reached through the TEB (`%gs`: a TLS slot from `TlsAlloc`, or `ArbitraryUserPointer`), not `%fs`, which Wine's own C library holds.
- std.platform's words `__win_io` (a read's or a write's count) and `__win_memory_info` (`VirtualQuery`'s answer) are the process's, which one worker allows: they become each worker's (thread-local) with the workers.
- No stack-overflow report yet; the main stack is reserved by the linker (`/stack:125829120`). A frame larger than a page needs a probe on Windows ([[MYLA-717]]).

## What changed

**Each thread's state** ([[selfhost/codegen.my]], [[selfhost/ir_x86.my]]). With tasks, a thread's block (`__tls_blocks`, as on Linux) is the value of a TLS slot of its TEB: `_start` takes one (`TlsAlloc`), checks it is one of the TEB's 64 (`%gs:0x1480 + 8 * index`; else `runtime error: no thread-local storage slot left`) and keeps its offset in `__win_tls_off`; each thread stores its block's address there. A variable that is a thread's own is two loads away (`win_tls_block`: the offset, then `%gs:(offset)`), where Linux has one `%fs:` operand: `tv_at` gives the lines and the operand (`Inst.Global`, the executor's `__exec_run` and `__task_fail`), `Intrinsic.RtAddr` computes the address in %rax, and such an address is a temporary of its own instead of a load's or store's operand (`remat_texts`; `rt_var_at` lost its Windows case). std.platform's `__win_memory_info` and `__win_io` are each thread's, in the 56 bytes the block had free after its lists (`tls_offset` 4744 and 4792; `TLS_SIZE` unchanged), and without tasks the process's words as before.

**The workers** ([[selfhost/runtime_x86.my]]). `_start` calls `__workers_count` (`MYLA_THREADS`, else std.platform's `cpus_allowed`: the bits of `GetProcessAffinityMask`'s process mask), maps the rings for that many, and `__start_workers` (`emit_windows_start_workers`) makes each worker a thread of `CreateThread`, 8 MiB of stack reserved as Linux's workers have, running `__win_worker`: counted started, its block in its TLS slot, its stack guarantee, then `__exec_run`. The handles are closed; a `CreateThread` that fails leaves the workers made so far, as Linux's `clone` does. The rings are `map_memory`'s (reserved and committed, 8 MiB a worker: commit charge, not memory, until a ring is used; Linux's are `MAP_NORESERVE`).

**Parking** ([[lib/std/platform_windows.my]]). `park(addr, seen)` is `WaitOnAddress(addr, &seen, 4, INFINITE)` (the expected value in the thread's `__win_io`, as a park allocates nothing) and `unpark_one` `WakeByAddressSingle`. kernel32 doesn't export them (nor does Wine's), so `windows_start` finds them (`find_synch`) in the DLL of their API set, `api-ms-win-core-synch-l1-2-0.dll`, or else kernelbase.dll (`GetModuleHandleW`, `GetProcAddress`), into `__win_synch`; where the system has neither, a park is a nap of a millisecond. The poller (timers only until [[MYLA-706]]) waits on a word of its own with the nearest timer's time limit, which `poller_wake` sets and wakes, so a frame pushed or a nearer timer wakes a polling worker as epoll's eventfd does on Linux.

**A stack overflow** (D216). `_start` adds a vectored exception handler (`__win_on_fault`) before anything else, in every program, and each thread keeps 64 KiB of its stack for it (`SetThreadStackGuarantee`, as large as Linux's alternate signal stack). `EXCEPTION_STACK_OVERFLOW`, or an access violation within 64 KiB below or 1 MiB above the faulting stack pointer (Linux's test), is reported once (`__segv_once`): `runtime error: stack overflow` on the standard error, then `__exit_program(1)` with its exit hook; a second thread sleeps until the first one's exit ends the process. Anything else goes on to the system (`EXCEPTION_CONTINUE_SEARCH`).

**The heap's aligned mappings.** `unmap_memory` decommits what it is given and releases the whole mapping once none of it is committed (the region at the allocation's base reserved and reaching its end, `VirtualQuery`). So the chunk-aligned mapping of a big cell, whose ends `std.rt.map_aligned` gives back at once, goes back whole when the cell is freed, and std.rt is unchanged; before, each such cell left its 1 GiB-and-more reserved for good. The ends are committed until they are given back, which costs commit charge for a moment, not memory.

**The exit.** `exit` waits (at most a second) until every worker thread made has started (`__win_threads`): a thread still starting when the process ends makes Wine print `wine client error:…: sendmsg: Socket operation on non-socket` on the standard error. Found by `test_generated_widths` and `test_generated_extremes` under `test-target.py` (a runtime error at once, 4 workers); reproduced by 40 such programs at once on 16 workers (8 such lines among their outputs), none in 120 after.

**Tests** ([[tests/burn/test_windows.py]]): `Workers` (a stack overflow on the main thread and on a worker, `MYLA_THREADS` workers, big cells given back whole, the concurrency stress programs on 1, 4 and 16 workers), `StackProbes` and `FramesOnWindows` ([[MYLA-717]]); `test_tasks_on_one_worker` is `test_tasks`. `_targets.py`: `test_children_run_in_parallel` isn't excused on Windows any more.

## Proposals (accepted 2026-11-15: the first is D222, the other two stand as built, D227)

1. **D221 ends: Windows runs tasks on workers as Linux does** (built). Proposed row, superseding D221: *"Windows runs its tasks on workers as Linux does ([[MYLA-703]], superseding D221): threads of `CreateThread`, `MYLA_THREADS` or one per CPU of the process's affinity mask, at most 32; each thread's state block is the value of a TLS slot of its TEB (`TlsAlloc`, one of the 64 at `%gs:0x1480`), not `%fs`, which Wine holds; they park with WaitOnAddress and WakeByAddressSingle."* Alternatives for the block: the TEB's `ArbitraryUserPointer` (`%gs:0x28`, one load instead of two, but a word the system's loader and debuggers may write too), or the executable's static TLS (a `.tls` section and `_tls_used`, the loader allocating each thread's block: also two loads, `%gs:0x58` then index 0, and the PE's TLS directory to write).
2. **WaitOnAddress found at the start, no second import library** (built): `GetModuleHandleW` of the API set's DLL, then kernelbase.dll, and `GetProcAddress`, both kernel32's (D212 holds: no ntdll). Alternative: an import library for `api-ms-win-core-synch-l1-2-0.dll` (what Microsoft's `Synchronization.lib` and Rust's std link), which needs the driver's second import library, wave 4's with ws2_32 and bcrypt; with it this becomes two `__imp_` slots and `find_synch` goes. Or kernel32's SRW lock and condition variable for parking, which wakes the wrong waiter once two words are parked on.
3. **A Windows stack overflow is also an access violation next to the stack pointer, and the handler keeps 64 KiB** (built): Linux's test, for a fault past the guard page (a frame without its probe, or a thread whose guard is spent), and Linux's alternate stack's size. Alternative: only `EXCEPTION_STACK_OVERFLOW`, the guarantee the system's default, which a report through the exit hook may not fit in.
