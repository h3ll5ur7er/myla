## What was built

- **H6, named frame slots** ([[selfhost/interp.my]]): a function's outermost scope (`RtFrame`) has two slots, `env` (the environment its type parameters are bound in, D172) and `caller` (the call whose position a `#track_caller` function reports), where both were hidden variables that wanted the frame's first place: `%caller` took it, so `frame_env` found no `%env` and a generic `#track_caller` function's operators were untyped. `frame_env`, `frame_caller`, `bind_types`, `bind_caller`, `note_caller`, `failure_at`; a lambda still captures its frame's environment (`eval_lambda`, `call_closure`).
- **H13, constants** ([[MYLA-715]]): a constant is evaluated when it is first named (`const_value`, `const_state`), in a frame of its own, so one made of another module's constant or of one declared after it is right; they were evaluated in declaration order, the entry module's before the library's, and such a name read as unit. **The start-up follow-up** comes with it: the constants nobody names are never evaluated, and the float literals of the modules every program loads were 8.3 M of an empty program's 35.7 M instructions (below). The rest of the creep is the implicit `std.float`'s check and load, D258's ([[MYLA-796]], [[MYLA-793]]); nothing here touches them.
- **M12**: a deferred block that fails as a case returns is the case's failure, as it is a program's (`run_defers`): `[panic("in defer")]` and `[exit(5)]` pass interpreted as natively; the blocks after it unwind from there.
- **M2, the record**: std.rt's `test_record` builds the record in the head's cell, which `std.test`'s `reporting` now makes `RECORD_ROOM` (4,096) bytes long, and writes it at once: a pipe never cuts a write of at most PIPE_BUF bytes, so a worker's `eprint` lands before or after it. A longer one is written in pieces after the other workers stopped (`panic_report_tasks`, `record_in_pieces`). **The forged record**: a child a signal ended has no record (`case_outcome`), and a case's `process.args()` holds the program only, as interpreted (`reporting` ends the runtime's argument vector after it), so the nonce isn't handed to the case either (the Low item).
- **D259, a case's time limit**: `myla test --timeout <s>`, 60 by default, 0 for none (`selfhost/main.my`, `TestOptions.timeout`, `DEFAULT_TIMEOUT`); the verdict `timed out after 60 s` (std.test's `timed_out`), whatever the case expected, alike on both runners. Interpreted: a deadline looked at every 1024 blocks (`tick`), at each turn of the async scheduler and of an async loop, and every sleep and idle wait bounded by it (`nap`); a case past it stops as a failure does but runs no deferred block. Natively: std.test's `output_within` runs each child, reading its streams as they come, and kills it past the limit; on Linux each child leads a process group of its own (`own_group`, setpgid), which the runner kills as the case ends or passes the limit, and has `PR_SET_PDEATHSIG` (SIGKILL), as the runner has towards `myla test` (`dies_with_parent`): killing `myla test --native` leaves nothing behind. `myla test` passes the limit to the harness (MYLA_TEST_TIMEOUT) and runs a wasm32 harness through `output_within`, the limit counted from each case's `B:0;` (`resumed_tests` in [[selfhost/driver.my]]).
- **The run-time spec that doesn't fit**: std.fmt's new `spec_misfit(spec, class, found)`, which native code (`builtin_format` in [[selfhost/lower.my]], the name in `backend_std_functions`) and the interpreter check at `x.format(spec)`: `show(2.5, "x")` is `runtime error: the format spec 'x' needs an integer, found 'float64'` at the call, the hole's message; it was ignored on every runner. `spec_error` stays, for the seeds' code.

## Proposals (the owner decides)

1. **The limit's default is 60 s, in whole seconds** (built; `--timeout 0` none, a day at most). Alternatives: no limit unless asked (a spinning case hangs CI, which D259 is against), a shorter default (10 s: a case of std.compress under qemu or Wine takes seconds), fractions of a second.
2. **A timed-out case is `timed out after <n> s` whatever it expected, and runs no deferred block, interpreted as natively** (built). Alternatives: run its deferred blocks interpreted (they may spin too, and natively the child is killed), or `expected …, timed out` for a case expecting a panic or an exit.
3. **The case's process group is killed when the case ends too, not only past the limit** (built, Linux): nothing a case started outlives it, and a child of its that holds the case's output doesn't keep the runner reading. Alternative: kill the group only past the limit or when the runner dies, so that a case may leave a daemon behind (the interpreter leaves it).
4. **A child a signal ended has no record** (built), and the case doesn't get the nonce in its arguments. Alternatives: hold each record's kind to the status (`P` only with 0, `X:<s>` only with `s`), which a `--from` run's statuses (0 for every case but the last) can't give; or a nonce in the environment, which a case reads as easily.

A decision row for the integrator: "**A test case's limit** ([[MYLA-792]], D259): 60 s by default, `--timeout <s>` in whole seconds, 0 for none; past it the case is `timed out after <n> s` whatever it expected and runs no deferred block, on both runners. Natively, on Linux, each case's child leads a process group of its own, killed when the case ends or passes the limit, and dies with the harness, which dies with `myla test` (`PR_SET_PDEATHSIG`); a child a signal ended has no record (D191), and a case's `process.args()` is the program only" | "D259 asked for a default and the group kill; 60 s keeps a slow case under an emulator inside it; the group's kill at a case's end keeps a case's own children from holding the runner, and the record rule closes the forged `P:0;` of the 2026-11-17 review's M2".
